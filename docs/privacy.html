Refuel Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-08-09 · Applies to Refuel 1.0.0 (Windows app, Android app, web dashboard)

Summary

No account, no login, no analytics, no ads, and no collection of your code or prompts. Refuel reads usage logs that already exist on your own machine and keeps everything local unless you explicitly turn on phone sync.

1. What is read

  • The developer collects no personally identifying information — no name, email, or account.
  • The Windows app reads the local log files your AI coding tool already writes, and extracts only token counts, timestamps and the tool's name. Your code, conversations, prompts and API keys are never read or transmitted.
  • Log files are opened read-only and are never modified.

2. What leaves your device (only if you enable phone sync)

  • Phone sync is off by default. Nothing leaves your PC until you turn it on.
  • What is sent: token counts, timestamps, agent names, and your chosen accent colour. That is all.
  • The payload is end-to-end encrypted with AES-GCM and relayed through ntfy.sh, an open-source push relay. The relay only ever sees ciphertext. The decryption key exists only on your PC and your phone.
  • The channel is a 166-bit random secret topic. The key is transferred only inside the QR code's URL fragment (#), which browsers never send to any server.
  • You can turn phone sync off at any time, and regenerating the topic and key immediately invalidates any existing pairing.

3. No advertising, no analytics

This release contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs. The Android app requests no advertising identifier. Nothing about your behaviour is recorded or transmitted for any purpose beyond showing you your own usage.

4. Android permissions

  • Internet — only used to poll the relay when phone sync is on.
  • Camera — used only while you are scanning the pairing QR. No image is stored or transmitted.
  • Notifications — to show limit-reset alerts.
  • Exact alarms — so a reset alert fires at the right minute instead of being delayed by power saving. You can revoke it in Android settings.
  • Boot completed / wake lock — required by the notification component to restore scheduled alerts after a reboot.

5. Storage and deletion

  • Settings and history live only on your own devices: ~/.refuel on the PC, and the browser storage of the phone app.
  • Uninstalling the app and deleting that folder removes everything. Nothing is retained on any server.
  • Messages passing through the relay are transient and are not archived by the developer.

6. Third-party services

  • ntfy.sh — the relay, used only when phone sync is on. It receives ciphertext only.
  • GitHub — contacted once a day to check for a newer release (read-only, no account). This can be turned off in settings.

7. Open source

The full source is public so you can verify all of the above yourself: github.com/nohseongmin/Refuel
